# OneHealthID — Sovereign Health Intelligence Platform

**The "Aadhaar for your body" — One identity, one record, total sovereignty.**

OneHealthID is a unified health record system designed to eliminate the fragmented, paper-based medical landscape in India. It empowers citizens to own their health data, allowing them to carry a single, sovereign National Health ID across every hospital, clinic, lab, and pharmacy.

## The Problem
India's healthcare system is crippled by data fragmentation. Patients carry physical files between hospitals for their entire lives, leading to lost critical records, delayed diagnoses, and preventable medical errors. This information gap represents a massive systemic crisis that technology solved a decade ago for finance (UPI) and identity (Aadhaar)—OneHealthID brings that same revolution to healthcare.

## Core Features
- **Unified Health ID**: A single, lifelong identifier that follows the citizen across the entire healthcare ecosystem, making fragmented paper records obsolete.
- **Biometric Intelligence Engine**: Real-time computation of clinical-grade insights including **VO2 Max**, **HRV**, **Recovery Score**, and **Metabolic Age** from wearable data.
- **DPDP-Compliant Privacy Layer**: A sovereign data-sharing architecture where patients—not hospitals—grant, control, and instantly revoke data access per doctor or facility.
- **Lifetime Medical Vault**: An encrypted document storage system for prescriptions, lab reports, and discharge summaries—accessible in under 3 seconds anywhere in India.
- **Real-Time Analytics**: Visual health tracking using **Chart.js** to monitor wearable trends, workout frequency, and heart rate zones.

## Tech Stack
- **Backend**: Node.js & Express.js
- **Database**: MySQL (Complex 7-table schema for health fragmentation management)
- **Frontend**: EJS Templating, Vanilla CSS (Custom Design System)
- **Data Visualization**: Chart.js 4.4
- **Storage**: Multer-based encrypted file vault

## Why it Matters
OneHealthID isn't just a database; it's a **Sovereign Health Intelligence Platform**. By unifying data, we enable predictive diagnostics and ensure that life-saving information is always in the pocket of the patient, especially during the "Golden Hour" of medical emergencies.
